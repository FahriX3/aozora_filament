## Brand & Style
The design system delivers an energetic, modern Japanese anime gaming aesthetic infused with traditional cultural pride. Designed for high school extracurricular clubs, the visual experience balances educational clarity with the vibrant, layered flair of contemporary anime game interfaces (such as Genshin Impact, Persona 5 Tactica, and Blue Archive).

The atmosphere is optimistic, adventurous, and youth-focused. The user interface achieves high emotional resonance by combining clean white surfaces and cloud geometries with deep navy indigo structure, electric sky blue gradients ("Aozora" — azure sky), and vermilion/torii red accents. Subdued cultural motifs—such as stylized seigaiha waves, cloud cutouts, and torii-inspired line frames—act as structural anchors rather than superficial decor.

The design movement mixes **Modern Japanese Anime UI** with **Frosted Tactile Surfaces**:
- Razor-sharp typographic hierarchy paired with soft, rounded container geometry.
- Translucent floating panels with delicate dual borders (0.5px vermilion or frosted azure borders).
- Interactive card micro-interactions featuring floating badge tags, dynamic kanji watermarks, and subtle paper/cloud layering.

## Colors
The color palette represents the transition from dusk to dawn beneath an open sky. 

- **Primary (`#0D59F2` - Vivid Royal Blue)**: Represents youth vitality, school pride, and the boundless potential of the sky. Used for major calls-to-action, active state markers, and glowing accent rims.
- **Secondary (`#0E1326` - Indigo Night)**: Grounding dark blue that replaces standard harsh grays. Used for strong headers, primary navigation banners, and high-contrast night-mode containers.
- **Tertiary (`#E83A30` - Torii Vermilion)**: Cultural accent drawn from traditional Shinto shrine gates and celebratory Japanese stamps (Hanko). Reserved for high-urgency notifications, featured tags, cultural activity badges, and accent pips.
- **Neutral (`#F4F7FC` - Cloud Mist)**: A cool, crisp off-white tinted with faint cyan-blue undertones, preventing optical fatigue while retaining a refreshing anime sky mood.

### Named Colors
- `aozora-sky` (`#38BDF8`): The signature vivid sky-blue used for secondary buttons, gradient stems, badge backgrounds, and link hovers.
- `torii-vermilion` (`#E83A30`): High-energy visual pops, Japanese stamp badges, and urgent highlights.
- `indigo-night` (`#0B1021`): Deepest background tier for gaming cards and dark footer bands.
- `cloud-white` (`#FFFFFF`): Clean surface card fills and floating chip backgrounds.
- `sakura-tint` (`#FDE8E8`): Soft pastel alert fills and cultural pill containers.
- `gold-shrine` (`#F59E0B`): Star achievements, tournament trophies, and honorary pins.

## Typography
The typographic architecture blends modern geometric warmth with crisp anime game HUD legibility.

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers friendly yet punchy rounded geometric forms that mimic contemporary Japanese light novel branding and anime companion apps. Dense weights (700, 800) create bold titles that pair cleanly with large Japanese Kanji glyphs (e.g. `青空`).
- **Body Copy (`Be Vietnam Pro`)**: Highly legible, open humanist proportions engineered for multi-lingual screen rendering (Indonesian Latin text alongside inline Japanese kanji, hiragana, and katakana readings).
- **Labels & Micro-data (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides an anime UI/tech flair for operational metadata, schedule badges, romanized rubies, level trackers, and school registration stamps.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- CJK Kanji titles should accompany primary headers using an opacity of 0.25 in the background as an artistic watermark, or stacked directly beside Latin text with a colored tag enclosure.
- Always apply uppercase transformation to `label-badge` and `label-md` tokens when annotating categories or club levels (e.g., `JLPT N5`, `CULTURE TRACK`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system follows a responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop transitioning to a 4-column flow on mobile, structured around a strict 8px base rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12-column grid, `max-width: 1280px`, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Layouts highlight asymmetric game-like profile hero panels and 3-to-4 card pillar spreads.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Cards reflow into paired 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 0.875rem`. Cards shift to single-column swipe carousels or stacked vertical feeds.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Component Breathing Room**: Interactive containers use internal padding of `space-lg` (1.5rem) down to `space-md` (1rem) on mobile.
- **Section Pacing**: Vertical spacing between thematic sections (e.g., from *Tentang Aozora* to *Kegiatan Kami*) utilizes `space-2xl` (4rem) to maintain clear mental divisions and visual pace.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is realized through clean anime HUD translucency, tinted ambient light glows, and crisp Japanese motif boundary outlines rather than muddy drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Pure base tint `#F4F7FC` with faint diagonal lattice patterns or seigaiha geometric watermarks at 4% opacity.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a subtle tinted edge: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(13, 89, 242, 0.08)`. Outlined with a 1px border of `rgba(13, 89, 242, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Active & Floating Modules)**: Overlaid panels and hovering action cards receive an elevated azure backlight: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(13, 89, 242, 0.16)`. Border color sharpens to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Popovers)**: Translucent frosted glass effect (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`, with a double-ring perimeter shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 16, 33, 0.25)`.
- **Vermilion Accent Elevation**: High-impact cultural elements (such as Torii stamp tags or CTA badges) cast a dedicated warm glow: `0 6px 18px -2px rgba(232, 58, 48, 0.35)`.

## Shapes
The shape system strikes a balance between sleek modern software ergonomics and playful manga bubble geometry. 

- Standard components (buttons, input boxes, surface cards) use **Roundedness Level 2** (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline).
- Feature cards and hero showcase containers scale to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to preserve a soft, friendly silhouette.
- Specialty tag badges, indicator pills, and Japanese kanji markers utilize full pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create collectible, sticker-like interface elements.
- Decorative cloud brackets and card corners occasionally incorporate subtle 45-degree chamfers or torii-style horizontal crossbars along their top borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Aozora Sky)**: Solid `#0D59F2` background, text in `#FFFFFF`, with `0.5rem` border-radius. Features a slight inner top highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`) and an icon slot (chevron, arrow, or Japanese sun icon). On hover, shifts to `#38BDF8` with dynamic glow `0 8px 24px rgba(13, 89, 242, 0.35)`.
- **Vermilion Action (Torii / Join Now)**: Vibrant `#E83A30` fill with white bold text. Used specifically for registration, joining the club, and primary callout triggers.
- **Secondary Ghost Button**: Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in `#0D59F2`, text in `#0D59F2`. Hover fills with `rgba(13, 89, 242, 0.06)`.
- **HUD Pill Button**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), white background, 1px border `rgba(14, 19, 38, 0.1)`, with emoji prefix or Japanese glyph accompaniment.

### Badges & Chips
- **Kanji / Culture Tag**: Pill-shaped element, `#FDE8E8` fill, `#E83A30` text, bold `label-badge` typography. Contains bilingual microcopy (e.g., `🇯🇵 BUDAYA`).
- **Status Indicator**: Translucent blue pill (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`), text `#0D59F2`, carrying a glowing dot indicator for live routine activities (e.g., `● SETIAP KAMIS 16.00`).

### Cards & Feature Modules
- **Feature Pillar Card (Interactive Learning, Culture, Community, Achievements)**: White background (`#FFFFFF`), `1.5rem` corner radius, internal padding `space-lg`. Features an elevated emoji or vector icon framed within an octagonal or circular gradient container at the top left. Bottom edge is stamped with a faint kanji watermark (`学`, `友`, `勝`) at 6% opacity.
- **Program Activity Card**: Multi-layered card featuring an upper visual badge, title in `headline-sm`, and bulleted roadmap tags. Includes subtle top border accent in Torii vermilion or Aozora sky.

### Input Fields & Forms
- **Text Inputs**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `rgba(14, 19, 38, 0.12)`, `rounded-md`, typography `body-md`. On focus, transitions to border `#0D59F2` with ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 89, 242, 0.18)`.
- **Placeholder**: Tinted slate-gray (`#94A3B8`) with contextual examples in both Indonesian and Japanese romaji.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square shape with `4px` radius for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- Unchecked: White surface with 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border.
- Checked: `#0D59F2` fill featuring a crisp white geometric checkmark or central vermilion pip.

### Lists & Activity Tracks
- Unordered lists replace standard dots with custom stylized mini-shuriken, cherry blossom pips, or sky-blue arrows (`→`).
- Item lines feature comfortable spacing (`space-sm` gap) with alternating subtle zebra hover tints (`rgba(13, 89, 242, 0.03)`).

### Unique Domain Components
- **Hanko Seal Badge**: A circular or square stamp framed in `#E83A30` vermilion containing club authenticity stamps (SMKN 1 Purwokerto approval mark).
- **Kanji Floating Watermark**: Ultra-large typography positioned behind section heroes, dynamically scaling with responsive viewport width.
